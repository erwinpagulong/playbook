## 1. On the "75% win rate" request

**No honest analysis can guarantee a 75% win rate** — anyone who promises that is selling something.

- **Win rate ≠ profitability.** With a 2:1 reward:risk, you only need **~35% wins** to break even and **~45–50%** to profit consistently. A "75% win rate" usually comes from taking tiny profits while letting a few losers run — a *losing* expectancy setup.
- The probabilities below are **subjective estimates from the current chart**, not calibrated guarantees. Treat them as a directional edge, not a promise.
